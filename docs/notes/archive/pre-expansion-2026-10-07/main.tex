\begin{abstract}
We would like to investigate whether an uncertainty set around a learned process reward
can reduce reward hacking under inference-time search or reinforcement learning.
The challenge is to cover relevant reward errors after optimization while
retaining useful discrimination between reasoning paths. These notes distinguish
established findings from proposed methods, identify close prior work, explain
the necessary foundations, and specify possible empirical and theoretical approaches.
\end{abstract}
